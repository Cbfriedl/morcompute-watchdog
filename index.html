How a provider actually earns MOR

A bid is a standing offer: I will serve model X at P wei per second. A consumer opens a session against a bid and the provider accrues P × duration — paid for wall-clock time held, not for tokens produced. You then claim it on chain.

Two constraints shape everything. The protocol clamps P between 1e10 and 1e16 wei/s (0.000864 – 864 MOR/day), and you are paid only while headroom remains — headroom being stake − limitPeriodEarned. Run it to zero and the node keeps serving, and keeps paying its upstream bill, while earning exactly nothing.

There is no annual maximum on what you can earn. providerRegister adds to stake and never touches limitPeriodEarned, so claiming and re-staking reopens headroom 1:1 and earning resumes. Total income is unbounded; what is bounded is how much can be earned between re-stakes. The useful measure is therefore return on the MOR staked, not a ceiling.
