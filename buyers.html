Avg bid is MOR paid divided by session-days (MOR ÷ seconds held ÷ 86400), which recovers the price per day actually paid. It is deliberately not the mean of the bid prices on offer, nor an average across sessions: a 20-minute hold and a 24-hour hold each count as one session while the second is 72× the spend. Bidders is how many live bids compete for that model network-wide — a present-tense figure standing beside historical purchases, so it moves when the book moves.
